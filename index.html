Things I've made

AbujaDirect:

AbujaDirect is a civic-tech platform that helps residents of Gwarinpa, Abuja report community issues, access government service information, and track the progress of reported problems. The goal is to improve transparency, accountability, and communication between citizens and government agencies.

SolanaGuard AI:

SolanaGuard AI is a read-only Solana transaction security assistant. It retrieves real Devnet transaction data, explains what the transaction appears to do, scores transparent security signals, and uses Backboard AI to make the findings easier to understand.
